# Brag Plan: OpenCloser

## What is this app?
OpenCloser is a free, open-source desktop app that puts an entire AI sales team — strategist, lead researcher, voice caller, coach, manager — on your machine, including a War Room where the AI makes live cold calls.

## The angle
Blockbuster trailer for the AI sales team that lives on your desktop. Play the scale completely straight — sweeping statements, slam-in titles, a hero product moment — because the absurd part is that it's all real: the app actually dials prospects, reads their sentiment live, and coaches you mid-call. The trailer treatment is the joke that isn't a joke.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen. Two lines slam in, one after the other: "SDR software costs $1,000/month." — beat — "Ours costs $0." The second line is the hook; the price contrast earns the next 18 seconds.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The wordmark slam: "OpenCloser" builds to full screen with "THE OPEN SOURCE AI SDR" beneath it, dashboard screenshot blooming behind.
- The flow, performed live: pipeline kanban → cursor clicks Sarah Jenkins's lead card → lead detail opens → cursor hits INITIATE AUTONOMOUS EXECUTION → War Room opens on a live call with sentiment bars and a SPIN coaching tip. The product doing its thing, not describing it.
- The stat volley: three cards fire in — "5 AI AGENTS", "100% LOCAL", "$0 FOREVER".

## Outro / punchline
"OpenCloser." Full-screen lockup. "Download free." + github.com/issacops/opencloser. Cut to black. No wink.

## User flow worth showing
Pipeline kanban → click lead card → lead detail → INITIATE → War Room live call with sentiment + coaching. (Entry → key action → result: pick a lead → start the AI call → watch it sell with live telemetry.)

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: blockbuster trailer for the AI sales team that lives on your desktop
- Interpretation: dramatic trailer pacing and scale — sweeping statements, slam-in type, full-bleed hero UI — played completely straight because the product backs every claim; restraint only in that nothing winks.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — a steady bed under trailer-scale reveals, swelling gently into the War Room moment and the outro
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady and clean, 110 BPM — best bundled fit for cinematic)
- Music treatment: full bed 0–21s at 0.3–0.4, gentle fade-out over the last 1.5s; let the final lockup ring, not the music
- Music cue guidance: preset at assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json (tempo 110); strong-cue targets — War Room reveal → 8.74s; stat cascade → 15.84s / 17.47s; outro slam → 19.66s; beat grid (~0.55s spacing) for the stat-card cascade and type beats
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the hero window glow and the wordmark presence breathe with RMS/bass. No waveforms, no equalizers, no pulsing text
- SFX posture: moderate, cinematic — 2-3 big ones plus motion-matched UI clicks
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Hook price slam — impact hit on "$0"
  - Wordmark slam — deep bell reveal
  - Flow clicks (lead card, INITIATE) — mouse clicks matched to the simulated cursor
  - Stat cards — card-place hits in cascade
  - Outro lockup — final bell, then silence
- Restraint rule: sound must never talk over the product — no SFX under dense UI motion, no sting that outlasts its moment, music never above 0.4
